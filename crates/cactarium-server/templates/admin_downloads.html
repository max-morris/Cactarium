Files a sim pins by sha256 (a tarball component, an input's download), submitted with cactup download submit <url>. The Cactarium fetches each once in the background and checks its sha256; it keeps no copy. Only a verified download can be decided. A sim pinning a download that isn't approved isn't published: an upload is refused, a tag waits, and a tag pinning a rejected download becomes an invalid version. Trusted users' and admins' submissions are approved once verified.

A rejected download can be reopened (back to pending) or approved. Sim tags that became invalid over it stay invalid until read again: just cli reread <admin> <owner/name> <version> (or Admin, Entries, Re-read invalid version).
